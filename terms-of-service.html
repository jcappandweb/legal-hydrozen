2. Description of the Service

HydroZen is a personal hydration tracking application for Android. Its core features include:

  • Logging daily water and beverage intake
  • Personalized daily hydration goal calculation based on your profile, activity level, and local weather conditions
  • Scheduled drink reminders and notifications
  • Hydration insights and history charts
  • Home-screen widget for quick logging
  • Optional synchronization with Android Health Connect

3. Data We Collect and How We Use It

3.1 Health & Activity Data (Health Connect)

With your explicit permission, HydroZen reads and writes the following data types through the Android Health Connect API:

  • Hydration records (read & write)
  • Body weight (read)
  • Exercise sessions (read)
  • Step count (read)
  • Sleep sessions (read)

This data is used solely to compute your adaptive daily hydration goal and to populate the Insights screen. It is processed on-device and is never uploaded to our servers.

3.2 Location & Weather

With your permission, HydroZen accesses your approximate device location to retrieve current weather conditions from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (api.met.no). Weather data is used exclusively to adjust your daily hydration goal (e.g. higher goals on hot days). Your precise location coordinates are not stored or shared beyond the weather API request.

3.3 Preferences & Logs

Your profile settings, hydration logs, reminder schedules, and daily goals are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore and Room. We do not host a backend server and do not have access to this data.

3.4 Analytics & Crash Reporting

HydroZen uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google) to collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. This helps us identify bugs and improve the App. No personally identifiable information is included in these reports. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
